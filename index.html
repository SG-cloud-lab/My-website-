The Bleeding General: The Cost of Leading in the Midst of Private Pain

Every general faces battles, but a leader is denied the luxury of a public collapse. When you carry the weight of leadership, your wounds must remain invisible. You pull the cloak of grace tight around your shoulders—not out of pride, but because you know that if the general panics, the army scatters. This book is a raw, unvarnished revelation and a holy invitation to step into the wilderness, take off the heavy armor, and weep freely before the only One who requires no performance. "The call to lead is not the absence of pain, but the choice to carry it for the sake of others."
